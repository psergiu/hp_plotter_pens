# HP Plotter Pens

3D-printed HP plotter pen models, using ballpoint pen refils.
Pros:
* easily available refills in multiple colors
* very thin lines
* less prone to dry out than ink pens
* much more writing distance per pen compared to ink pens
Cons:
* requires thicker paper else ball might not roll and leave ink on paper
* not suitable for points of very short lines (<1mm)
* very thin lines (not suitable for filling polygons or thick lines)

## Compatibility
The pens are compatible with the follwing plotters: 
* HP 9872
* HP 7440A ColorPro
* HP 7225
* HP 7470A
* HP 7475A
* HP 7550 (can be used with full-size uncut refills)
* HP 7090A Measurement Plotting System
* DEC LVP16
* IBM 7372
* IBM 6180

## Included models
### Bic Cristal refills
To be used with refills taken out of from Bic Cristal pens, available in multiple colors. Example: 
* https://www.amazon.com/BIC-MSBAPP241-Ballpoint-Assorted-24-Count/dp/B075CGYY5P/
* https://www.amazon.com/BIC-Cristal-Multicolour-Ballpoint-Point/dp/B07LF68GPR/

Cut off refill tube 3-4 mm above top of printed pen body - if you used white PETG, you can smear ink from the cut-off session on top of pen for easier identification. Warning: messy

### "Beadable Pen" refills
Those are short refills for the so-called "Badable" DIY pens. Available in black, blue & red in packs of 100+. Example:
* https://www.amazon.com/PASISIBICK-Plastic-Beadable-Refills-Writing/dp/B0CYLHZYT6/

The ink level in the refill tube is usually at the perfect so the tube can be shortened without causing a mess.
