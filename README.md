# HP Plotter Pens

3D-printed HP plotter pen models, using ballpoint pen refils.
Pros:
* easily available refills in multiple colors
* very thin lines
* less prone to dry out than ink pens
* much more writing distance per pen compared to ink pens
Cons:
* requires thicker paper else ball might not roll and leave ink on paper
* not suitable for points of very short lines (<1mm)
* very thin lines (not suitable for filling polygons or thick lines)

The pens are compatible with the follwing plotters: 
* HP 9872
* HP 7440A ColorPro
* HP 7225
* HP 7470A
* HP 7475A
* HP 7550 (can be used with full-size uncut refills)
* HP 7090A Measurement Plotting System
* DEC LVP16
* IBM 7372
* IBM 6180
